Common AC Problems We Check

Quick breakdown of frequent air conditioner issues diagnosed during doorstep technician visits in Coimbatore.

Problem 01

1. AC Not Cooling

The indoor blower fan blows room-temperature air without cooling. This commonly stems from choked dust filters, a weak outdoor compressor capacitor, or blocked condenser fins.

Problem 02

2. AC Water Leakage

Water drips or overflows from the indoor unit down bedroom walls. This is usually caused by algae sludge clogging the condensate drain line or an unlevel indoor wall plate.

Problem 03

3. AC Not Starting

The unit shows no power response from the remote or display switch. The technician checks voltage from the stabilizer, the internal PCB fuse, and power wiring continuity.

Problem 04

4. AC Making Noise

Unusual rattling, squeaking, or buzzing sounds from the indoor blower or outdoor fan. Technicians examine blower wheel balance, motor shaft bearings, and bracket mounting cushions.

Problem 05

5. Low Airflow

Air throw from the louvers feels very faint even at maximum fan speed. Evaporator fins choked with dust or a dust-laden blower wheel are inspected and cleared.

Problem 06

6. Outdoor Unit Problem

The indoor unit runs but the outdoor condenser fan or compressor never engages. The technician checks communication wiring, contactor relays, and start/run capacitors.

Problem 07

7. Remote / Sensor Problem

The AC does not register remote commands despite fresh batteries. The technician inspects the indoor display receiver board, IR sensor, and manual button panel.

Problem 08

8. Temperature Cutoff Issue

The AC turns off before the room reaches temperature or runs continuously without cycling. Room thermistor and copper coil temperature sensors are tested with a multimeter.

Problem 09

9. Drain Line Clog

Stagnant condensate in the drain tray produces musty odors and moisture. Technicians flush the drain pipe with pressure and sanitize the collection trough.

Problem 10

10. Refrigerant-Related Issue

If cooling is severely degraded and thin copper discharge tubes show frost, the technician tests operating pressure with manifold gauges before identifying any leak or recharge needs.

Common AC Problems During Summer in Coimbatore

During the intense peak heat months, these are the most frequent issues Coimbatore households report:

Summer Issue 01

AC Cooling Kammiya Irukku

When outdoor temperatures cross 39°C, an AC with dusty filters or choked outdoor condenser fins struggles to exchange heat. Other potential causes include a weakened compressor capacitor, reduced blower speed, or refrigerant issues. A technician inspects all factors before drawing conclusions.

Summer Issue 02

AC Water Leakage Inside Room

High indoor humidity produces heavy condensate water. If the drain trough or outlet pipe has accumulated algae, dust, or mold slime, the water cannot escape and overflows down the wall. Cleaning the drain line restores normal drainage.

Summer Issue 03

AC Making Heavy Noise

Continuous operation puts stress on rotating parts. Loose cabinet screws, worn fan motor bushes, or blower wheel imbalance produce rattling, buzzing, or squeaking noises that require mechanical tightening and lubrication.

Summer Issue 04

AC Frequently Switching Off

If the outdoor unit is exposed to direct blazing sun with poor ventilation, the compressor can overheat and trip its internal thermal overload protector. Voltage fluctuations and faulty temperature sensors can also cause rapid cutoffs.

Summer Issue 05

Ice Formation on Coils & Tubes

Frost or ice forming on the indoor cooling coil or thin copper tube indicates severely restricted airflow (such as heavily choked air filters or a stalled blower) or a refrigerant circuit problem. Running an icy AC can harm the compressor, so early inspection is advised.

Summer Issue 06

AC Running But Room Not Cooling

The indoor fan blows air and the outdoor fan spins, but room temperature stays unchanged. This can be caused by unengaged compressor relays, poor room insulation, open doors, or a technical fault in the sealed refrigeration circuit.

Low Cooling Does NOT Automatically Mean Gas Filling Is Required!

A common misconception among homeowners is that whenever cooling feels low, the AC immediately needs "gas filling". In reality, an air conditioner operates on a closed hermetic loop — refrigerant does not get consumed or evaporate unless there is a physical leak. Low cooling is frequently caused by choked filters, a failing capacitor, or blocked condenser fins. Our technician conducts a full diagnostic check and measures gauge pressures before advising any refrigerant service.

Simple AC Maintenance Tips for Coimbatore homes

Keep your air conditioner running smoothly with these practical practices:

  • Clean air filters every 2–3 weeks: Remove the front plastic mesh filters, wash under running tap water, dry in shade, and reinsert.
  • Keep outdoor unit clearance: Ensure at least 2 feet of open space around the outdoor condenser for unimpeded heat discharge.
  • Do not block indoor airflow: Keep curtains, wardrobes, and tall wall decor away from the indoor air intake and discharge louvers.
  • Attend to water drips immediately: Never ignore indoor drips; addressing a choked drain line early prevents wall plaster damage.
  • Inspect drain outlet outside: Check that the outdoor drain pipe end is not submerged in mud, potted plants, or blocked by garden debris.
  • Use best thermostat setting: Setting the thermostat between 24°C and 26°C delivers comfortable cooling with balanced power consumption.
  • Schedule pre-summer servicing: Have a professional technician perform a deep jet wash and electrical check before peak summer begins.
