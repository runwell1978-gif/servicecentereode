Common Bosch Washing Machine Problems We Check in Coimbatore

If your Bosch washing machine shows any of these common symptoms, our technicians inspect the components to identify the exact cause:

Bosch E18 / F18 Drain Fault

Machine stops with water inside drum and beeps with E18 code. Caused by coin, safety pin, or lint blocking the front drain pump filter.

Bosch E17 / F17 Water Supply Error

Machine cannot take enough water within the specified time limit. Caused by clogged inlet filter screens or a faulty dual solenoid valve.

Bosch E23 / AquaStop Leak Detected

Water has collected in the bottom base tray, activating the float safety switch to prevent room flooding. The leak source must be checked.

Bosch Door Locked / Will Not Open

Cycle completes but the door remains locked or the plastic handle feels loose. Caused by a failed bi-metal interlock switch or broken latch.

Bosch High Pitch Drum Bearing Noise

A loud rumbling or jet aircraft sound when the drum spins at 1000–1400 RPM. Caused by worn out rear drum ball bearings and water seal.

Bosch Machine Shaking on High Spin

The machine walks or vibrates aggressively during spin. Caused by worn friction shock absorbers underneath the outer tub.
