Quick Summary: What You Need to Know

  • Air Conditioner: Clean nylon mesh filters every 2 weeks during peak summer; ensure outdoor unit has at least 2 feet of open air clearance.
  • Refrigerator: Maintain 4 inches of clearance from kitchen walls for heat dissipation; wipe door rubber gaskets monthly with mild soap.
  • Washing Machine: Clear the bottom coin trap filter monthly; use descaling powder every 30 days to counter borewell mineral hardness.
  • LED & Smart TV: Always use a surge protector strip; never spray cleaning liquids directly onto the display glass.
  • Kitchen Appliances (Microwave, Geyser, RO, Chimney): Replace RO sediment filters every 9–12 months, descale geyser heating elements annually, and degrease chimney baffle filters monthly.

1. Air Conditioner Care: Dust & Peak Summer Heat in Erode

From March through July, daytime temperatures in Erode routinely touch 38°C to 41°C. Air conditioners run 10 to 14 hours daily. Fine dry dust stirred up along major roads accumulates rapidly on the outdoor condenser fins and indoor filters.

When filters choke, cooling drops drastically and power bills jump. Washing filters every 2 weeks and scheduling an annual chemical jet foam service ensures your compressor runs without thermal tripping during the hottest afternoons.

2. Refrigerator Maintenance: Preventing Food Spoilage in Hot Weather

In our warm climate, a malfunctioning refrigerator can spoil hundreds of rupees worth of milk, curds, and vegetables in a single day. The most frequent issue is the freezer working fine while the bottom compartment stays warm, usually caused by a failing defrost heater or choked air damper.

Keeping the fridge 4 inches away from the kitchen wall and ensuring the rubber door gasket seals tightly prevents heat accumulation and compressor overload.

3. Washing Machine Health: Tackling Cauvery Basin Hard Water

Whether you use a front load, top load, or semi-automatic washer, ground water in many Erode localities contains dissolved calcium and magnesium salts. This hard water forms a chalky white crust around heating elements, drain pump impellers, and drum bearings.

Cleaning your coin filter monthly, checking rear hoses for kinks, and running an empty hot-water drum cleaning cycle with descaling powder extends machine life by years.

4. Television & Display Protection: Voltage Surges and Screen Care

Smart LED televisions are sensitive to voltage spikes and improper cleaning. Backlight burnout (sound working, screen dark) and motherboard boot loops are the top two TV repair requests across Erode.

Always power your TV through a dedicated surge strip. When cleaning the screen, spray glass cleaner lightly onto a microfiber cloth—never directly onto the glass, as liquid easily runs down inside the bottom bezel and corrodes display ribbon tracks.

5. Kitchen Appliances: Microwave, Water Geyser, RO Water Purifier & Chimney

• Microwave Oven: Clean food splatters quickly; sparking inside is usually caused by a grease-soaked mica waveguide cover sheet (an inexpensive ₹250 replacement).
• Water Geyser: Hard borewell water encrusts the copper heating element within 2 years. Descaling the tank prevents element burnout and saves electricity.
• RO Water Purifier: Replace sediment and carbon pre-filters every 9 to 12 months to protect the expensive RO membrane from fine sand and mud carried in borewell supplies.
• Kitchen Chimney: Wash baffle filters in hot caustic water every month to prevent greasy oil drops from dripping back onto cooking stoves.

When You Should Stop and Call for Professional Repair

Certain symptoms require immediate attention from a technician with insulated diagnostic tools to prevent permanent damage or safety hazards:

  • Burning electrical smell, sparks, or smoke from any appliance.
  • The appliance body gives a tingling electric shock when touched.
  • The appliance repeatedly trips your household MCB circuit breaker.
  • Major cooling or heating failure that does not resolve with basic filter cleaning or reset.
  • Unusual loud grinding, screeching, or banging mechanical noises.
