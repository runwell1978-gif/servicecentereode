Thermostat Issue

Single Door Freezer Box Over-Freezing

Starter Relay

Compressor Clicking Every Few Minutes

A clicking sound is heard from behind the fridge every 2 to 3 minutes, but the cooling motor fails to run.

Probable Cause: Burnt PTC starter relay or open overload protector
Technician Check: Tests relay resistance and compressor winding health
Resolution: Replaces starter relay and overload protector
Defrost Choke

Double Door Lower Cabin Warm

Freezer freezes water properly, but the lower compartment has no cooling and food spoils.

Probable Cause: Mechanical defrost timer or bimetal failure causing iced coil
Technician Check: Tests defrost timer motor and measures heater resistance
Resolution: Replaces defrost timer and clears ice blockage
Drainage Issue

Water Leaking Beneath Vegetable Tray

Defrost water overflows into the vegetable tray rather than draining into the rear compressor pan.

Probable Cause: Defrost drain hole choked with dust or food particles
Technician Check: Inspects drain trough and rear outlet hose
Resolution: Cleans and flushes drainage line with warm water
Door Seal

Door Gasket Loose with Cold Air Escape

Cold air escapes along the door frame, causing high electricity bills and moisture buildup.

Probable Cause: Rubber gasket hardened, cracked, or lost magnetic grip
Technician Check: Inspects seal contact around the entire perimeter
Resolution: Replaces magnetic rubber door gasket
Refrigerant Leak

Continuous Motor Running Without Chill

The compressor runs warm continuously, but the interior shelves remain completely room temperature.

Probable Cause: Pinhole gas leak in copper tubing or capillary choke
Technician Check: Checks suction pressure and inspects brazed joints
Resolution: Brazes leak spot, pulls vacuum, and recharges gas

BPL Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Coimbatore.

Hermetic Reciprocating Compressor

The primary cooling pump in BPL refrigerators. If it makes loud vibrations, gets extremely hot, or trips the power MCB, it needs thorough testing.

PTC Starter Relay & Overload Protector

Supplies initial power to the compressor start winding. If it burns out, the compressor clicks every few minutes without starting.

Direct Cool Rotary Thermostat

Regulates cabin temperature in single door models. If the internal bellows fail, the fridge either freezes everything or stops cooling.

Evaporator Fan Motor

Pushes chilled air into the lower compartment. When the fan motor seizes from moisture, the lower cabin stays warm.

Bimetal Defrost Switch

Closes the circuit to activate the defrost heater when frost forms. If faulty, the cooling coil becomes choked with solid ice.

Defrost Heater Element

Melts frost off the cooling coil so air can circulate. If broken, solid ice blocks the cooling vents.

Cabinet Door Gasket

Seals the door edges tightly against the steel cabinet. If the rubber is hardened or torn, cold air escapes continuously.

Defrost Drain Hose

Carries defrost meltwater away from the cooling coil. If choked with dust or food particles, water leaks inside the fridge.

When Should You Call for BPL Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
