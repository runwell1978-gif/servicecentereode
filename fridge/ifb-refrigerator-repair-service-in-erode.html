IFB Refrigerator Parts We Check or Replace

Simple explanations of essential components tested during our doorstep service visits in Erode.

Metal Cooling Inverter Compressor

Regulates cooling gas pressure across metal-backed cooling panels. If the compressor hums without starting, it needs testing.

PTC Starter Relay & Overload Protector

Protects the compressor motor from power surges. If burnt, the compressor clicks repeatedly without turning on.

Evaporator Air Circulation Fan

Pushes chilled air through multi-vent towers. If the motor slows down or seizes, cooling in the fresh food section drops.

Defrost Sensor (NTC Thermistor)

Measures frost levels on the cooling coil. If faulty, the coil chokes with ice and air circulation stops.

Main Inverter Control PCB

Controls compressor rpm, temperature sensors, and fan speeds. If damaged by voltage spikes, the fridge will not switch on.

Defrost Heating Element

Melts frost off the cooling fins during automatic defrost. If broken, solid ice blocks the cooling vents.

Magnetic Door Gasket

Keeps outside warm humid air from entering. If torn or loose, cold air escapes and frost accumulates on door edges.

Defrost Drain Pipe & Tray

Channels meltwater away from the cooling coil. When blocked, water collects on the bottom floor of the fridge.

When Should You Call for IFB Refrigerator Repair?

Look out for these warning signs to avoid food spoilage and costly compressor replacements.

  • ❄️ Cooling becoming low: Fresh food or milk spoiling quickly on lower shelves.
  • 🧊 Freezer not freezing: Ice cubes not setting or ice cream melting inside the freezer.
  • 💧 Water leakage: Water pooling beneath the vegetable crisper or leaking on the kitchen floor.
  • 🧱 Excess frost buildup: Dense ice accumulating on the freezer back wall, choking air vents.
  • 🔊 Compressor noise or clicking: Repeated clicking sounds every 2 to 3 minutes without cooling.
  • 🚪 Door not sealing: Moisture droplets forming around door frames due to weak magnetic gaskets.
  • ⚠️ Repeated tripping or blinking: Fridge cutting power or digital display flashing error codes.
  • ⚡ Unusual electrical smell: If you smell burning insulation, switch off the appliance immediately, unplug the cable, and call for inspection.
