Common Samsung TV Problems Repaired in Coimbatore

Frequent issues faced by Samsung television owners in Coimbatore and how our local technician inspects them.

Power Circuit Fault #1

Samsung TV Clicking Sound & Not Starting

Problem Observed: Continuous clicking noise comes from the rear panel every few seconds, the red standby LED blinks twice, but the screen never turns on.

Why This Happens: The power supply relay tries to start the TV, but detects a shorted secondary diode, failed capacitor, or an overloaded backlight circuit on the BN44 board.

Technician Inspection: Technician measures the 13V DC standby rail, isolates the mainboard load, and tests the secondary rectifier diodes on the power board.
Backlight Failure Fault #2

Audio Plays Normally but Samsung Screen is Dark

Problem Observed: Dialogue and channel sound play loud and clear, but the picture is completely missing. Faint images appear under a mobile flashlight.

Why This Happens: One or more LED diodes in the backlight strips have burned open, breaking the circuit and shutting off display illumination.

Technician Inspection: Uses an external LED tester to check individual strip bars and verifies constant-current driver output from the power board.
Smart OS Fault #3

Samsung TV Stuck on Tizen Startup Logo

Problem Observed: The screen freezes indefinitely on the 'Samsung Smart TV' logo, or powers off and reboots every 10 seconds in an endless loop.

Why This Happens: Corrupted system partition on the eMMC flash chip, failed automatic firmware update, or unstable core power rail on the mainboard.

Technician Inspection: Accesses service mode to reset cache, tests 1.1V and 1.8V processor rails, or reflashes the Tizen firmware.
Display Timing Fault #4

Colored Vertical Lines Across Samsung Display

Problem Observed: Thin green, pink, or multi-colored vertical lines run from top to bottom across the picture, sometimes with image jumping.

Why This Happens: Failing T-Con timing controller IC, oxidised LVDS ribbon pins, or moisture damage to the panel's Chip-on-Film (COF) bonds.

Technician Inspection: Cleans and reseats LVDS ribbon cables, measures VGH and VGL voltages on the T-Con board, and inspects panel edge bonding.
Wireless Network Fault #5

Samsung TV Wi-Fi Turned Off / Cannot Connect

Problem Observed: Network menu displays 'Wi-Fi is turned off', network toggle cannot be switched on, or TV disconnects after 5 minutes.

Why This Happens: Dust accumulation or power rail failure at the bottom-mounted Wi-Fi/Bluetooth module connector beneath the bezel.

Technician Inspection: Opens the lower bezel casing, cleans the ribbon contact pins, measures 5V supply, and replaces the module if chip is defective.
Sound Issue Fault #6

Samsung TV Speaker Rattle & Distorted Dialogue

Problem Observed: Sound crackles or vibrates loudly during dialogue or news reading, sounding like torn paper inside the back cover.

Why This Happens: Acoustic speaker paper cone surround has torn due to humidity and heat, causing the voice coil to scrape the magnet.

Technician Inspection: Tests speaker impedance with a multimeter (standard 6-ohm or 8-ohm) and installs a matched replacement stereo speaker pair.

Samsung TV Repair Parts & Components

Essential internal components tested and serviced for Samsung televisions in Coimbatore.

Every television uses distinct circuit boards and display parts. Here is an overview of key Samsung TV parts, their function, and the problems they cause when faulty:

Samsung SMPS Power Supply Board Power Circuit

Converts 230V AC mains into stabilized 13V DC for the motherboard and high DC boost voltage for Crystal 4K and QLED backlights.

Fault symptoms: Relay clicking continuously inside rear panel, 2-blink red indicator, TV restarting every 5 seconds.

Samsung Main Logic Motherboard Core Logic

Houses the Samsung Crystal or Quantum processor, Tizen OS firmware, eMMC memory, and HDMI switch controllers.

Fault symptoms: Smart Hub freezing, TV stuck on Samsung Smart TV logo screen, continuous reboot cycle, Wi-Fi disabled.

Samsung High-Luminance LED Backlight Strips Display Backlight

Direct-lit or edge-lit LED strips that illuminate the Crystal 4K or QLED liquid crystal display layer.

Fault symptoms: Audio plays clearly but screen remains completely dark, bright white dots on display from fallen diffuser lenses, dim screen corners.

Samsung T-Con Timing Controller Board Display Timing

Translates video data from the Samsung Tizen board into high-speed column and row addressing pulses.

Fault symptoms: Double image jumping vertically, screen split into light and dark halves, or colored vertical lines.

Samsung Backlight Boost Regulator Circuit Backlight Regulator

Provides high-frequency PWM dimming and constant current regulation to Crystal 4K LED strings.

Fault symptoms: Rapid screen flickering, one side of panel blinking, or protection circuit tripping into 2-blink standby.

Samsung Internal Stereo Acoustic Drivers Audio Driver

Down-firing acoustic enclosures producing balanced stereo output for news, dialogue, and films.

Fault symptoms: Vibrating speaker buzz during speech, muffled treble, or completely silent audio output.

Samsung HDMI 2.0 / 2.1 & USB Port Connectors Input Interface

Interface ports connecting set-top boxes, gaming consoles, and soundbars to Samsung televisions.

Fault symptoms: Set-top box displays 'No Signal' across all HDMI ports, port physically wobbly, or ARC audio dropping.

Samsung Wireless Wi-Fi & Bluetooth Transceiver Wireless Module

Internal transceiver card mounted below bezel connecting Tizen OS to home Wi-Fi and Smart remote.

Fault symptoms: Wi-Fi option disabled or grayed out in Network settings, Smart Remote failing to register, pairing failure.

Samsung Standby LED & IR Remote Photodiode Remote Sensor

Front infrared receiver eye and red standby status LED mounted at center bottom bezel.

Fault symptoms: TV ignores remote commands even with new batteries, red standby LED unlit, or slow remote response.

Samsung Display Panel High-Speed Ribbon Cables Signal Ribbon

High-density flat ribbon cables linking the Tizen motherboard directly to the display glass source boards.

Fault symptoms: Picture jittering horizontally, intermittent lines when TV is moved, or solarized color noise.
