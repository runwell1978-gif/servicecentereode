Backlight Failure Fault #1

Dialogue Audible but Hyundai Screen is Pitch Black

Problem Reported: Channel audio and music play normally, but the display stays completely black. Shining torch reveals ghost pictures.

Failure Analysis: Burnt LED diode in direct-lit backlight string opens electrical circuit, triggering booster driver shutdown.

Action Taken: Checks forward voltage of each backlight strip with bench analyzer and replaces entire array with brand-matched strips.
Smart OS Fault #2

Hyundai TV Stuck on WebOS / Android Logo

Problem Reported: Television turns on, displays the Hyundai logo, and freezes indefinitely without loading the WebOS dashboard.

Failure Analysis: System storage partition corruption resulting from unfinalized background firmware update.

Action Taken: Boots into WebOS service console, clears partition cache, and reflashes authorized Hyundai operating system.
Power Circuit Fault #3

Hyundai TV Dead Standby Light Following Surge

Problem Reported: Power cord is connected to mains, but front indicator light does not illuminate and TV does not respond.

Failure Analysis: Sudden electrical surge ruptured primary glass fuse, shorted input rectifier, and damaged chopper transistor.

Action Taken: Replaces blown primary fuse, checks bridge rectifier diodes, and repairs switching regulator on power board.
Audio Issue Fault #4

Hyundai Internal Speakers Distorted and Rattling

Problem Reported: Dialogue sounds muffled with heavy cabinet vibration during loud vocal passages or music.

Failure Analysis: Prolonged vibration and humidity have deteriorated the speaker cone edge, causing voice coil contact.

Action Taken: Tests speaker driver impedance, removes defective speaker pair, and installs genuine Hyundai acoustic drivers.
Display Timing Fault #5

Colored Vertical Lines Down Hyundai Screen

Problem Reported: Fine vertical colored scanlines stripe the display from top to bottom while audio continues normally in the background.

Failure Analysis: T-Con board gamma IC failure, corrosion on flexible flat cable contacts, or partial gate driver separation.

Action Taken: Cleans flat ribbon leads with electrical contact cleaner, checks T-Con VGH/VGL/VCOM voltages, and examines gate driver tabs.
Remote Link Fault #6

Hyundai Magic Remote Fails to Re-pair

Problem Reported: Magic Remote cursor disappears from screen, scroll wheel is unresponsive, and pairing notification fails.

Failure Analysis: Bluetooth RF transceiver desynchronization on mainboard or degraded wireless antenna power line.

Action Taken: Executes Magic Remote hardware reset sequence, re-registers RF handshake, and confirms 3.3V power to Bluetooth PCB.
